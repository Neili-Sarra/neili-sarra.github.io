Embedded weather station

STM32 firmware for a small weather station: temperature and pressure from a BMP180, a light alarm on an LDR and a 16x2 LCD, all built around two prioritized interrupts. Written in C with the STM32 HAL and simulated online.

  • ADC analog watchdog on the LDR, high priority: crossing the threshold turns on the LED and buzzer at once.
  • Push button on EXTI, medium priority: starts a 60 s measurement campaign sampled at 1 Hz by TIM3.
  • BMP180 and LCD share the I2C bus; the LCD always shows the latest reading.

Built with STM32 Nucleo-C031C6, C, STM32 HAL, STM32CubeIDE, NVIC, EXTI, ADC, TIM3, I2C, BMP180, LCD 1602

    Smart Bin

    A bin that sorts waste on its own. A camera and a YOLOv8 model classify each item as recyclable, organic or non-recyclable, a stepper motor turns a tilted tray to the right compartment, and a Flutter app shows how full each compartment is.

    • YOLOv8 trained with transfer learning on Kaggle GPUs, on two merged datasets (TACO and Household Trash) whose 48 classes we remapped into 3 categories.
    • An IR sensor starts each cycle; a finite-state machine drives the camera, the stepper motor, LEDs and buzzer, and three ultrasonic sensors measure each compartment.
    • Raspberry Pi 4 architecture designed down to the GPIO map and level shifters, then validated on a PC and Arduino prototype over UART that sorts in under one second.
    • Flutter app reads a Flask REST API every 2 s: fill gauges, an alert above 80% and the last detection with its confidence.

    Built with YOLOv8, Python, TensorFlow Lite, Raspberry Pi 4, Arduino, UART, Flask, Flutter, HC-SR04, 28BYJ-48 stepper, SolidWorks, CATIA

    Cooling water monitor

    Real-time ESP32 firmware for a cable cooling system. A float sensor reports the water level, a 5 V relay module drives the pump, and a web dashboard linked over MQTT lets operators follow the level, get alerts and start or stop the pump remotely.

    • Automatic pump control from the float sensor replaced manual threshold checks and made the system more reliable.
    • The ESP32 and the dashboard exchange data over MQTT: live level, threshold alerts and a history chart.
    • Operators can take over from the website at any time and switch the pump on or off remotely.

    Built with ESP32, float level sensor, 5 V relay module, MQTT, Wi-Fi, web dashboard
